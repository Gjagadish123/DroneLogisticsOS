Airspace & safety

Built for Dubai's airspace

No-fly zones

DXB, DWC, Al Minhad and Zabeel are geofenced. Routes bend around them automatically.

Altitude layers

Eastbound at 90 m, westbound at 120 m, so opposing traffic never shares a level.

Drone separation

Drones closer than 160 m across and 22 m vertically are separated by an automatic climb.

Manned aircraft

ADS-B traffic is tracked. Drones near a helicopter drop to 60 m.

Temporary closures

Close an area on the map and every affected route is re-planned live.

Weather and faults

Sandstorms recall the fleet. A faulty drone lands and its order is re-dispatched.
