How it works

One delivery, end to end

01

Order placed

From the customer app or a merchant's system

02

Drone assigned

Nearest pod with a drone that has payload and battery for the trip

03

Pickup

The winch loads the parcel at the merchant without landing

04

Delivery

Geofenced route, then the parcel is lowered at the door

05

Recharge

Back to a pod, or to the Energy Hub for a swap

The whole loop runs without a pilot. Operators step in only by exception, from the command center.
