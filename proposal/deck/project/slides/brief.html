The brief

Four things the network must do

Drone pods across Dubai

Nine rooftop pods with six pads each launch and recover drones close to demand.

Nearest-pod auto-dispatch

Each order goes to the closest pod holding a drone with the payload and battery for the whole trip.

Automatic battery swap

Drones route themselves to the Energy Hub before charge runs short. A robot swaps the pack in 60 seconds.

Super Admin monitoring

A live 3D map of every drone, pod, order and aircraft, with full airspace control.
